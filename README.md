## How the Cash Back Actually Lands in Your Account

This is the part where a lot of "is it legit?" questions come from, so it's worth walking through slowly.

When you shop through TopCashback — either by clicking "Get Cash Back" on a store page or by activating the browser extension — your transaction enters a three-stage lifecycle:

1. **Pending.** The transaction is tracked, but the retailer hasn't confirmed it yet. This stage exists because retailers wait out their return windows before locking in the sale.
2. **Confirmed.** The retailer has acknowledged the transaction, but the affiliate network hasn't sent the invoice yet.
3. **Payable.** The money is yours. You can cash out whenever you want, to whatever method you want, and the balance sits there until you decide.

How long does each stage take? It depends entirely on the retailer. Some fast-paying stores confirm within 24 hours. Some take weeks. A few (notoriously, certain travel and electronics retailers) can take months. Reddit users have reported occasional waits of a year or more on edge cases, though that's the exception, not the rule. TopCashback's own guidance is to check the "estimated payable time" on each transaction in your Earnings page, which gives you a retailer-specific expectation.

The honest take: this is the same lag every cashback site deals with, because the lag is on the retailer's side, not the cashback platform's. If a retailer is slow to confirm, every cashback app you use will be slow on that retailer.
